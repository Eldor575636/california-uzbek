Uzbekistan Beats India in Samarkand to Take Sole Lead at the Chess Olympiad

The team won all six of its matches; in round six it defeated defending champions India 2.5-1.5.

24 September 2026 · 1 min read · California Uzbek

Uzbekistan's men's chess team has won all six of its matches at the 46th Chess Olympiad in Samarkand, taking the sole lead without losing a single match.

The win over India

In round six the host team beat reigning world champions India 2.5-1.5. The decisive point came from Nodirbek Yakubboev, who beat Nihal Sarin after the Indian grandmaster blundered his queen in the key game.

The players

Javokhir Sindarov (rated 2778) scored 4 points from 5 games (three wins, two draws). The team also features Nodirbek Abdusattorov (2762), Shamsiddin Vokhidov (2654) and Mukhiddin Madaminov (2611).

What comes next

In round seven Uzbekistan faces two-time Olympic champions China. The tournament runs over 11 rounds under the Swiss system.

In brief

  • Tournament: 46th Chess Olympiad, Samarkand
  • Result: 6 wins in 6 rounds, sole lead
  • Key win: over India 2.5-1.5
  • Star: Javokhir Sindarov 4/5
  • Next opponent: China (round 7)
