President Shavkat Mirziyoyev visits Karshi State University: 14,000 students, 3 new dormitories, 7 laboratories

According to the Presidential Press Service, the head of state toured the university's internationally accredited programmes, joint degrees and research laboratories during a trip to Kashkadarya.

23 October 2026 · 1 min read · California Uzbek

President Shavkat Mirziyoyev visited Karshi State University during a working trip to the Kashkadarya region. According to the Presidential Press Service, the head of state reviewed the university's educational facilities, research laboratories, language centres and student services.

Students and faculty

The university has more than 14,000 students across bachelor's and master's programmes (full-time, part-time and distance), with a teaching staff of 579.

Dormitories and facilities

As part of last year's large-scale construction, three new dormitories added more than 1,300 places — said to have fully met housing demand. The campus also includes a 240-place kindergarten, a library, canteens, a youth centre and a 1,000-capacity media studio.

Accreditation and joint programmes

Four of the university's fields — English Philology, Mathematics, Applied Mathematics and Physics — hold international accreditation. The university runs 14 joint educational programmes with universities in Russia and Belarus, and operates five language centres (Chinese, German, French, English, Russian).

Research laboratories

The university has established an International Laboratories Center combining seven laboratories focused on artificial intelligence, machine learning and robotics. The visit is part of Uzbekistan's ongoing higher-education reform — expanding capacity, aligning programmes with international standards and strengthening STEM research.
