U.S.–Uzbekistan Trade Tops $1 Billion: Washington Deals and New Investment

Bilateral trade reached $881 million in 2024 (+15%) and exceeded $1 billion in 2025. At the 6 November 2025 Washington meeting, deals on Boeing aircraft, critical minerals and agriculture were announced.

19 September 2026 · 2 min read · California Uzbek

Trade and investment ties between the United States and Uzbekistan are expanding. According to Uzbek Minister Laziz Kudratov, bilateral trade reached $881 million in 2024 — a 15% year-on-year increase. According to the Caspian Post, in 2025 bilateral trade exceeded $1 billion.

2024 trade figures

According to figures cited by Minister Laziz Kudratov, Uzbek exports were $317 million in 2024, up 25%, while imports reached $564 million, up 10%. Total trade was $881 million.

The Washington deals (6 November 2025)

According to a November 2025 U.S. State Department statement, a package of deals was announced at the 6 November 2025 meeting in Washington between Donald Trump and Shavkat Mirziyoyev.

  • Boeing–Uzbekistan Airways: an order for up to 22 Boeing 787 Dreamliners worth about $8.5 billion; projected to support 35,000+ US jobs; deliveries reported to begin around 2031 (sources cite $8bn–$8.5bn).
  • Critical minerals / rare earths: a US–Uzbekistan investment agreement of up to $400 million, per the U.S. State Department (November 2025).
  • Agriculture: a commitment of up to $2 billion for US agricultural machinery, plus 2 million tonnes of US soybeans and 100,000 tonnes of US cotton over three years.

Mirziyoyev's guarantee

I assure you, I personally guarantee the success of US companies operating in Uzbekistan. At the Ministry of Investments, a dedicated Deputy Minister for US Affairs has been appointed. He will accompany you 24/7.

— Shavkat Mirziyoyev, President of Uzbekistan (New York, 22 September 2025)

300+ US companies

More than 300 US companies now operate in Uzbekistan — a figure that has doubled since 2017. They include GM, Boeing, Citi, JP Morgan, Cargill, Caterpillar, Honeywell, John Deere and Coca-Cola. On 9 June 2025, an MOU was signed in Tashkent between Uzbekistan's Ministry of Investments, Industry and Trade and the US Commercial Service.

Scale: US vs China

While trade with the US is growing, it remains far smaller than trade with China. Per Uzbek data, Uzbekistan–China trade in the first half of 2026 was about $7.7 billion, and per Chinese customs data about $8.87 billion (up 31% year-on-year). The C5+1 framework, in U.S. State Department framing, envisions over $100 billion in collective trade and investment with Central Asia.

By the numbers

  • 2024 total trade: $881 million (+15%)
  • Exports: $317 million (+25%)
  • Imports: $564 million (+10%)
  • 2025 total trade: over $1 billion
  • Boeing: 22×787 Dreamliners, ~$8.5bn, ~35,000 US jobs
  • Critical minerals: up to $400 million
  • Agricultural machinery: up to $2 billion
  • Soybeans: 2 million tonnes; cotton: 100,000 tonnes (3 years)
  • US companies: 300+ (doubled since 2017)
  • China trade (H1 2026): $7.7–8.87 billion
  • C5+1 plan: over $100 billion
