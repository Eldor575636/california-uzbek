President Shavkat Mirziyoyev meets FIFA President Gianni Infantino: Uzbekistan to co-host the 2027 U-20 World Cup

At the Tashkent meeting the two discussed Uzbekistan and Azerbaijan co-hosting the 2027 FIFA U-20 World Cup and accelerating the opening of a regional FIFA office in Tashkent.

30 September 2026 · 1 min read · California Uzbek

On September 30, President Shavkat Mirziyoyev met FIFA President Gianni Infantino in Tashkent. They discussed Uzbekistan and Azerbaijan co-hosting the 2027 FIFA U-20 World Cup and accelerating the opening of a regional FIFA office in Tashkent.

The 2027 U-20 World Cup

By a decision of the FIFA Council on October 2, 2025 (Zurich), the 2027 FIFA U-20 World Cup will be co-hosted by Uzbekistan and Azerbaijan. It is the 25th, anniversary edition of the tournament, featuring 24 teams from six confederations. The award makes it the first FIFA youth tournament co-hosted by countries from two different confederations (Azerbaijan — UEFA, Uzbekistan — AFC).

A FIFA office and infrastructure in Tashkent

The sides agreed to accelerate the opening of a regional FIFA office in Tashkent. During the visit, Infantino launched Uzbekistan's first FIFA Arena mini-pitch at School No. 90 in the Chilanzar district — part of FIFA's goal of 1,000 mini-pitches worldwide by 2031. Construction of a new 55,000-seat central football stadium in Tashkent, with three training pitches, began in November 2025.

Uzbek football on the rise

On June 5, 2025, Uzbekistan's national team drew 0–0 away to the United Arab Emirates in Abu Dhabi and qualified for a FIFA World Cup (2026) for the first time in its history. The cooperation talks also covered developing youth talent and the international training of coaches, referees and sports specialists.
