Gaza students head to Ireland after winning a visa battle

More than 60 scholarship students had their visas refused in July; after a High Court ruling, 48 were approved. Today 20 of them set off for Dublin.

23 November 2026 · 1 min read · California Uzbek

On Monday, 23 November 2026, 20 students from Gaza are due to set off to take up fully funded scholarships at Irish universities. It follows months of struggle and a court case over visas. The students travel from Gaza via Jordan to the Irish capital, Dublin.

The visa battle

The students had been offered fully funded scholarships at Irish universities and were expected to arrive in summer 2026. But in July, Ireland's Department of Justice refused the visa applications of more than 60 students, citing insufficient documentation and inadequate proof of an intention to return home.

The High Court ruling

The students challenged the decision in court. On 27 October, High Court judge Mr Justice Anthony Barr overturned the refusal in a lead case — that of Shaymaa Abudalu, admitted to an engineering degree at University College Cork (UCC) — finding 'significant and demonstrable errors on the part of the decision maker', and ordered reconsideration within ten days. On 19 November it emerged in court that 48 students had been approved on appeal in total.

By the numbers

A total of 48 students were approved. Of these, 20 are leaving today and 28 are still waiting for permission to leave. The students will study at University College Dublin (UCD — cybersecurity, artificial intelligence), University College Cork (UCC — engineering) and the Irish World Academy of Music and Dance at the University of Limerick. According to The Guardian, a second group is expected in late December.

Context

Leaving Gaza remains difficult even after the October 2025 ceasefire. According to Gisha, only 212 people left Gaza between 6 and 12 November. The Rafah crossing operates under heavy restrictions. For that reason, only some of the students whose visas were approved can travel within the set timeframe.
