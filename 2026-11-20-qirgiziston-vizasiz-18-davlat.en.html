Kyrgyzstan proposes visa-free entry for 18 more countries and 60-day stays

Kyrgyzstan's tourism agency has opened a draft resolution for consultation that would add 18 countries to the visa-free list and extend the permitted visa-free stay from 30 to 60 days within a 120-day period.

20 November 2026 · 1 min read · California Uzbek

On 18 November 2026 Kyrgyzstan's State Agency for Tourism Development opened a draft government resolution for public consultation. The document proposes adding 18 more countries to the list of states whose citizens may enter visa-free, and extending the permitted visa-free stay. The document is still a draft and has not been approved.

What is proposed

Under the draft, the visa-free stay would be extended from 30 days to 60 days within a 120-day period. The agency said the measure is aimed especially at mountaineers and long expeditions, to allow more time for acclimatization and travel.

Which countries

The 18 countries proposed for the list include Indonesia, the Philippines, Jordan, Morocco, South Africa, Venezuela, Nepal, Tunisia, Colombia, the Dominican Republic, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Peru, Panama, the Seychelles, Guatemala, Trinidad and Tobago, and Jamaica.

By the numbers

Countries to be added: 18. Visa-free stay: from 30 to 60 days (within a 120-day period). No entry-into-force date has been set — the document is still under consultation.

Context

According to the sources, the draft was developed after analyzing the visa policies of neighboring Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. Uzbekistan is mentioned only as a comparative example and is not a party to the measure.
