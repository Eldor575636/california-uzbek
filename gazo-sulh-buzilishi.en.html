Amid the Truce, an Israeli Drone Strike Kills One and Wounds Seven in Gaza

Gaza's Government Media Office says the ceasefire has been violated 4,379 times; the truce took effect in October 2025.

27 September 2026 · 1 min read · California Uzbek

Although a ceasefire is in effect in Gaza, Israeli strikes caused casualties on 27 September. In the Al-Mawasi area near Khan Younis in southern Gaza, a drone strike reportedly killed Uday Adli al-Assar, 28.

The day's toll

According to Anadolu, a total of seven people were wounded across several areas that day: one critically at the Al-Shati refugee camp, and five near the Sheikh Radwan market in northern Gaza City. Middle East Eye and Wafa reported three (Wafa: two, one critical) injured in the Al-Shati incident.

How the truce is being broken

Gaza's Government Media Office says the ceasefire was violated 4,379 times between 10 October 2025 and 24 August 2026. Territory health officials have cited more than 1,300 Palestinians killed since the truce took effect.

The wider picture

About 74,000 people have reportedly been killed and more than 175,000 injured in Gaza since October 2023. The US-brokered truce took effect on 10 October 2025; terms such as Hamas disarmament and a full Israeli withdrawal remain unresolved.

In brief

  • Where: southern Gaza (Al-Mawasi, Khan Younis) and Gaza City
  • 27 Sep: 1 killed (Uday Adli al-Assar, 28), 7 wounded
  • Truce violations: 4,379 (Oct 2025–Aug 2026)
  • Truce: in effect since 10 October 2025

Reported from Anadolu, Middle East Eye and Wafa; sources differ slightly on the scope of the incident.
