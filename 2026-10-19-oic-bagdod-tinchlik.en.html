Uzbekistan calls for peaceful conflict resolution at the OIC session in Baghdad

The 52nd session of the OIC Council of Foreign Ministers was held in Baghdad; Uzbekistan's delegation was led by First Deputy Foreign Minister Bakhromjon Aloyev.

18 October 2026 · 1 min read · California Uzbek

The 52nd session of the Council of Foreign Ministers of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) was held in Baghdad on 17–18 October 2026. Uzbekistan's delegation was led by First Deputy Foreign Minister Bakhromjon Aloyev. The OIC unites 57 member states.

Uzbekistan's appeal

Bakhromjon Aloyev stressed the need to resolve conflicts peacefully, through political and diplomatic means, dialogue and negotiations. He called for strengthening dialogue and solidarity across the Islamic world and set out Uzbekistan's position on the Palestinian issue.

Afghanistan and enlightened Islam

Uzbekistan called for support of Afghanistan's socioeconomic recovery and regional integration. The delegation also raised the promotion of enlightened Islam, science and cultural cooperation, and the use of Central Asia's experience in building regional trust and good-neighborly relations.

Meetings

On the margins of the session, Bakhromjon Aloyev held bilateral meetings with OIC Secretary-General Hissein Brahim Taha and representatives of a number of states.
