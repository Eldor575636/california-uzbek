COVID-19 and 2020: the mass return of Uzbek labor migrants

The 2020 pandemic triggered one of the sharpest crises in the history of Uzbek labor migration. Borders closed, thousands of migrants were stranded at the Kazakh border, and — according to a UN Development Programme survey — about half a million Uzbeks returned home over the year. Remittances suffered, by the World Bank's assessment, their sharpest fall since independence. A historical reference, facts only.

How government (national) debt works: deficits, bonds, and the debt-to-GDP ratio

A budget deficit is a single year's shortfall (spending exceeds revenue); national debt is the accumulated total of those deficits over the years. Governments borrow mainly by issuing bonds (government securities); debt is measured against gross domestic product (GDP). For reference: per the IMF, Uzbekistan's public debt was 32.6% of GDP at the end of 2024. A reference, facts only.

What hadith is and how it was collected: the science of hadith and Imam al-Bukhari

A hadith is a report of the sayings, actions, or tacit approvals of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) — the second primary source in Islam after the Qur'an. Each hadith has an isnad (chain of narrators) and a matn (text); scholars grade them as sahih, hasan, or da'if. Among the six main Sunni collections — the 'Kutub al-Sittah' — the most authoritative is the Sahih of Imam al-Bukhari, who was born in Bukhara in present-day Uzbekistan. A reference, facts only.

The Karshi-Khanabad (K2) air base and Uzbek–US military cooperation (2001–2005)

After the attacks of 11 September 2001, Uzbekistan granted the US military use of the former Soviet air base at Karshi-Khanabad (K2) to support Operation Enduring Freedom in Afghanistan. The base was used for logistics, search-and-rescue, and special forces; in March 2002 the two states signed a Declaration on Strategic Partnership. After the Andijon events of May 2005 and the ensuing diplomatic tension, Uzbekistan gave formal notice to vacate the base; US forces departed that November. A historical reference, facts only.
