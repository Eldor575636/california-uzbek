Uzbekistan's move to a bicameral parliament: the 2002 referendum and the creation of the Senate (2002–2005)

From 1995 Uzbekistan's Oliy Majlis was unicameral, with 250 deputies. In a referendum on 27 January 2002 voters were asked to make parliament bicameral and to extend the presidential term from five to seven years; according to official results both measures were approved. The bicameral Oliy Majlis took office on 27 January 2005 after the December 2004–January 2005 elections: a lower house, the Legislative Chamber (120 seats at first), and an upper house, the Senate (100 members). How the US Federal Reserve (the Fed) works

The Federal Reserve (the "Fed") is the central bank of the United States, created by the Federal Reserve Act signed by President Woodrow Wilson on 23 December 1913. It consists of the Board of Governors in Washington (7 members, 14-year terms) and 12 regional Federal Reserve Banks; monetary policy is set by the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC). Its dual mandate is maximum employment and stable prices (a 2% annual inflation target adopted in January 2012). Its main tool is the federal funds rate: at the 28 October 2026 meeting it was held at 3.75–4.00%. Based on official sources (federalreserve.gov).

Digital payments in Uzbekistan: how Uzcard, Humo, Click and Payme work

Uzbekistan's cashless payment system is built on two national card schemes — Uzcard (launched in 2004) and Humo (launched by the Central Bank in 2019) — plus the mobile apps linked to them, chiefly Click and Payme. Cards are linked to an app; users send peer-to-peer (P2P) transfers by card number, pay by QR, and settle utility and other bills. Since 2021 Uzcard and Humo have become interoperable. According to a KPMG study, cashless card payments reached about 72% of GDP in 2025. Based on official and reputable sources.

Polish refugees and the Anders' Army in Uzbekistan during World War II (1941–1942)

In July 1941 the Sikorski–Mayski agreement restored Soviet–Polish relations and opened the way to forming a Polish army in the USSR. Commanded by General Władysław Anders, this army was relocated in early 1942 to Uzbekistan and other Central Asian republics, with its headquarters at Yangiyul. Thousands of Polish civilians, including many children, also came south; hunger and a typhus epidemic took many lives. Between March and September 1942 the army and civilians — about 115,000–116,000 people in all — were evacuated via Krasnovodsk to Iran. Based on reputable historical sources.
