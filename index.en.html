Bukharan merchants in the Russian Empire: the Siberian «Bukharans» and the caravan trade (16th–20th centuries)

One of the lesser-known chapters of Uzbek and Central Asian migration is the communities of Bukharan merchants and settlers across the Russian Empire. Called «Bukharans» (bukhartsy) in Russian documents, this group in fact covered merchants from across Turkestan, not only from the city of Bukhara. From the 16th–17th centuries they settled in Western Siberia, drove the caravan trade, and were later absorbed into the Siberian Tatars. This guide draws solely on scholarly sources and treats dates as cautiously as the historians do.
